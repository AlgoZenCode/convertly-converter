# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Convertly is committed to protecting your privacy and the personal information you share with us while using our USD to INR converter.

## Information We Collect
We may collect the following information when you use our website:
- Exchange rate and conversion data used in the browser
- Recent conversion history stored locally in your browser using localStorage
- Browser and device information for technical diagnostics
- General usage data for product improvement

## How We Use Information
We use the information to:
- provide the USD to INR calculator and related features
- improve website performance and stability
- maintain a better user experience
- understand overall traffic and usability patterns

## Local Storage
Our site may store recent conversion history in your browser using localStorage. This helps you view recent conversions without sending that data to a server. You can clear this data at any time from your browser settings or by clearing recent conversion history in the app.

## Third-Party Services
We use the free Frankfurter API to retrieve exchange-rate data. This service may process request data necessary to deliver live exchange-rate values. We do not sell or trade your personal information.

## Cookies
We may use basic cookies or browser storage technologies to enhance site functionality, remember your theme preference, or store local history. These are not used for advertising or profiling.

## Data Security
We take reasonable steps to protect user information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

## Your Rights
You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of any personal information we may hold. You can also disable browser storage or clear it manually.

## Updates
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any material changes will be reflected on this page.

## Contact
If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@convertly.app.
